‎#  Coffee
‎
‎ 
‎The coffee plant is an evergreen shrub or small tree belonging to the botanical genus Coffea and the family Rubiaceae.
‎
‎
‎##  Scientific Classification:
‎
‎
‎**Scientific name:**  *Coffee arabica*
‎
‎
‎**Kingdom:**  Plantae (Plants)
‎
‎
‎**Family:**  Rubiaceae (Madder family)
‎
‎
‎**Genus:**  Coffea
‎
‎
‎**Main Species:**  Coffea arabica (Arabica) and Coffea canephora (Robusta)
‎
‎
‎## Morphological Details
‎
‎
‎**Growth Habit:**  Grows as an upright woody shrub or small tree. Unpruned wild trees can reach heights of 9 to 12 meters (30 to 39 feet), but farmed plants are usually pruned to 2 to 3 meters for easy harvesting.
‎
‎
‎**Leaves:**  Simple, glossy, dark green, and waxy leaves grow in opposing pairs along the stem.
‎
‎
‎**Flowers:**  Small, star-shaped, white or pinkish flowers bloom with a strong fragrance. They open after rainy periods and last only a few days.Fruit: Known as a "coffee cherry," the fruit is a round drupe that starts green and matures to bright red, yellow, or purple.
‎
‎
‎**Seeds:**  Each cherry typically holds two oval seeds with a flat face and a lengthwise groove, which are processed into the commercial coffee "bean".
‎
‎
‎## Description 
‎
‎
‎*Coffea arabica*  is a perennial plant cultivated for its
‎coffee beans.
‎
‎
‎##  Distribution
‎ 
‎
‎Coffee is cultivated in several parts of Ethiopia, including
‎areas of the southern and southwestern highlands.
‎
‎
‎##  Importance
‎ 
‎
‎Coffee has major agricultural and economic importance in
‎Ethiopia.
‎
‎
‎##  Uses
‎
‎
‎- Coffee production
‎- Local consumption
‎- Export
‎- Agricultural income
